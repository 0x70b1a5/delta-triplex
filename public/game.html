    Online features

    Daily puzzles and solve rankings come from deltatriplex.com, the game's own server. When you solve a daily or Endless puzzle, your time, rotations and a random player code are sent there. There are no accounts, ads or third-party trackers.

    Turn this off to keep everything on this device. Endless, Create and How to Play work offline; daily puzzles and rankings don't. You can change this any time in Settings.
